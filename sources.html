Countdown target

  • Release date: 19 November 2026, the latest date announced and the one Rockstar's own pages show today (17 Nov 2026). https://www.rockstargames.com/VI says “Coming November 19, 2026”, and the Newswire post https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/pre-load-now (10 Nov 2026) says “make sure you're prepared for November 19.” Newswire article pages returned Access Denied to automated fetches; the dates were read from the Newswire cards on the GTA VI site.
  • Unlock time: Rockstar's support article “Grand Theft Auto VI regional pre-load and release times on PlayStation 5 and XBOX Series X|S” (updated 3 Nov 2026) says, as quoted by outlets: “Grand Theft Auto VI will release midnight local time on November 19, 2026, in all regions for both PlayStation 5 and XBOX Series X|S.” The support site didn't respond to my fetch, so I'm relying on these cross-checks: PC Gamer https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-release-time-date/ , Polygon https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-release-time-pdt-edt/ , PlayStation US support https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/games/gta-vi-support/ (“midnight local time on November 19, 2026”) and the PlayStation Blog https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/ (rolling midnight launch by country).
  • Assumption: Rockstar gives no single exact worldwide instant, only local midnight, so the countdown uses 00:00 US Eastern on 19 Nov 2026. US daylight saving ends on 1 Nov 2026, so that is EST (UTC−5) = 2026-11-19T05:00:00Z (05:00 UK time). Outlets report the US PlayStation Store listing carries this same 05:00 UTC moment for the whole US store; players on the US West Coast may see 21:00 PT on 18 Nov. After that instant the card shows “GTA VI is out now”.
