Sources & credits: Neonshore

Last updated 18 Nov 2026. This build of Neonshore uses no official Rockstar Games images, fonts or logos.

Original artwork (Neonshore)

  • 3D scenes on the Community, Shop and Sunset Job pages (assets/js/hero3d.js): procedural geometry and shaders written for Neonshore. There are no third-party models or textures. The Shop and Sunset Job scenes float preview images of the Sunset Job pack, which is Neonshore's own artwork.
  • Illustrations (assets/art/*.svg), all original and generated by a Neonshore build script (kept in the source folder, not deployed):

  • Logo and favicon (assets/logo.svg, favicon.svg): original.
  • Sunset Job previews (shop/img/): the sale edition of the pack, all original Neonshore artwork. The branded edition is not for sale and is not included.

Official trailers (embedded, not hosted)

Each ID was checked on 17 Nov 2026 with YouTube oEmbed, which returned author "Rockstar Games" (https://www.youtube.com/@RockstarGames):

  • rz1B4B2yBZY: Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Trailer

The posters are original Neonshore illustrations, not frames from the videos.

News sources

The confirmed-news timeline links three Rockstar Newswire articles. Titles were checked on 17 Nov 2026, and the one-line summaries are our own paraphrase.

  • "Pre-Load Grand Theft Auto VI Now": https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21 (10 Nov 2026)
  • "Explore the Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Gallery": https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/39ak81277255o9 (5 Nov 2026, inferred)
  • "Grand Theft Auto VI Pre-Load Begins on November 10": https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712 (3 Nov 2026, inferred)

The two inferred dates come from the related-article dates on the pre-load page and from the article text. Rockstar's CDN blocked the rendered pages for those two articles.

Official pages linked: /VI, /VI/only-in-leonida, /VI/launch-gallery, /VI/music, /VI/media/screenshots, /VI/media/videos and /newswire, all on rockstargames.com.

Countdown

The countdown targets the US launch at 00:00 ET on 19 Nov 2026 (EST, UTC−5), which is 2026-11-19T05:00:00Z. Rockstar announces "midnight local time" in each region.
